# 01 — Stakeholders

---

## Player

### Profile
An athlete (typically age 10–25) who wants to train, compete, improve, and get discovered. Core user of the platform.

### Goals
- Find quality coaches and training sessions
- Track and improve performance metrics
- Earn recognition through XP, levels, ranks, and achievements
- Get discovered by better coaches and academies
- Connect with other athletes for motivation and community
- Manage training schedule and venue bookings

### Pain points
- No single place to track sports progress across coaches and venues
- Hard to find and compare coaches in their area
- No objective measure of improvement over time
- Lack of motivation without visible progress
- Difficulty managing multiple sessions, venues, and coaches
- No verified athletic identity or portfolio

### Daily workflow
1. Open app → check dashboard for today's session
2. View XP progress and achievements
3. Log performance metrics after training
4. Check messages from coach
5. Browse community feed during downtime
6. Book next session or explore new coaches

### KPIs
- Sessions attended per week/month
- Performance metric improvement (speed, stamina, technique, strength, agility)
- XP earned and level progression
- Achievement unlock rate
- Coach rating and feedback received
- Community engagement (posts, likes, comments)

### Success metrics
- Active days per week (DAU/MAU ratio)
- Session completion rate
- Metric logging frequency
- Coach retention (sessions with same coach)
- Achievement milestones reached

### Main screens
- **Dashboard**: Overview with KPIs, upcoming session, quick actions, performance chart, coach carousel, community preview, activity timeline
- **Performance**: Metrics chart with filterable dimensions, log new session, recent sessions list
- **Schedule**: Upcoming sessions list, session detail, reschedule/cancel
- **Achievements**: Level progress, rank, achievement grid with unlock states
- **Coach Discovery**: Search/filter coaches, coach cards, booking flow
- **Venue Discovery**: Search venues, sport filter, venue cards, detail view
- **Community**: Feed with posts, create post, like/comment/share
- **Chat**: Conversation list, chat detail, new conversation
- **Squad Goals**: Team goals, progress, member list
- **Video Analysis**: Upload video, AI analysis results, coach feedback
- **Profile**: Edit profile, theme toggle, help, about, sign out

### Permissions
- Create and manage their own player profile
- Book and cancel sessions with coaches
- Log performance metrics
- View and unlock achievements
- Post in community feed
- Send and receive messages
- Create and join squad goals
- Upload videos for analysis
- View and sign contracts (as party)
- View venues and book

### Future AI features
- AI-powered training recommendation based on performance trends
- Automated technique analysis from video upload
- Personalized session planner based on goals and progress
- Smart coach matching using performance data and learning style
- Injury risk prediction from workload metrics

## Coach

### Profile
A sports coach (Freelance or academy-affiliated) who trains athletes, runs sessions, and builds their coaching brand.

### Goals
- Find and attract new students
- Manage training sessions and schedules
- Track student progress and provide feedback
- Build verified reputation through ratings and reviews
- Increase income through more sessions and premium tiers
- Get placed at academies through the platform

### Pain points
- No central platform to manage students and sessions
- Difficulty marketing their coaching services
- No way to objectively demonstrate coaching effectiveness
- Payment collection and contract management is manual
- Hard to get discovered by academies for placement
- Students cancel or miss sessions without accountability

### Daily workflow
1. Open app → check today's session schedule
2. View student list with progress updates
3. Mark attendance for completed sessions
4. Provide post-session feedback and ratings
5. Respond to booking requests and messages
6. Manage availability and session slots
7. Check earnings dashboard

### KPIs
- Total students and active students
- Sessions conducted per week
- Student performance improvement (aggregate metrics)
- Rating and review score
- Session completion rate (vs. cancellations)
- Monthly earnings and subscription tier
- Academy placement status

### Success metrics
- Student retention rate
- Average rating (target: 4.5+)
- Session fill rate (capacity utilization)
- Revenue growth month-over-month
- Academy partnerships secured

### Main screens
- **Dashboard**: KPIs (sessions, students, earnings), upcoming sessions, student progress highlights, milestones
- **Schedule**: Session calendar, create session, session detail
- **Students**: Student list with profiles, performance data, attendance history
- **Earnings**: Monthly earnings chart, payout history, subscription management
- **Performance**: Student metrics overview, compare students, session feedback
- **Chat**: Conversations with students and academies
- **Profile**: Coaching profile, credentials, specialization, rates, availability

### Permissions
- Create and manage coaching profile
- Create and manage training sessions
- Accept/reject booking requests
- Mark student attendance
- Provide session feedback and ratings
- View student performance metrics
- Message students and academies
- Apply for academy jobs
- Receive and manage contracts
- Set availability and hourly rates
- Manage subscription tier

### Future AI features
- AI-powered student progress reports
- Automated session plan generation
- Optimal pricing suggestion based on demand and ratings
- Student attrition prediction
- Personalized coaching tip generation from performance data

## Academy

### Profile
A sports academy (e.g., cricket academy, football training centre) that manages multiple coaches, batches, and students.

### Goals
- Increase student enrolment and retention
- Recruit and manage quality coaches
- Track batch performance and coach effectiveness
- Build academy brand and reputation
- Place coaches in partner institutions
- Generate revenue through student fees and coach placement

### Pain points
- Manual management of batches, coaches, and students
- No centralized view of coach performance and student progress
- Hard to demonstrate academy effectiveness to parents
- Coach recruitment and placement is fragmented
- Limited visibility into marketing ROI

### Daily workflow
1. Open app → academy dashboard with overall KPIs
2. Review coach performance and attendance
3. Approve or manage batch allocations
4. Review placement pipeline and job postings
5. Check student enrolment numbers and trends
6. Manage marketing campaigns and coupons

### KPIs
- Total students enrolled and active
- Coach count and utilization rate
- Batch fill rates
- Student performance improvement (aggregate)
- Coach placement success rate
- Academy reputation score
- Monthly revenue

### Success metrics
- Student enrolment growth month-over-month
- Coach retention rate
- Parent satisfaction score (surveys)
- Placement rate for coaches
- Brand visibility (profile views, enquiries)

### Main screens
- **Dashboard**: KPIs (students, coaches, batches, placements), student growth chart, coach performance summary
- **Batches**: Batch list with coach assignments, capacity, schedule, student roster
- **Coaches**: Coach profiles, performance, attendance, placement status
- **Students**: Student list with progress, attendance, coach feedback
- **Placements**: Placement pipeline, coach job applications, invitations
- **Jobs**: Job postings, applicant tracking
- **Marketing**: Campaigns, coupons, promotions
- **Reports**: Revenue, enrolment trends, coach performance analytics

### Permissions
- Create and manage academy profile
- Create and manage batches
- Assign coaches to batches
- Invite and manage coaches
- View student data (with consent)
- Post job listings for coaches
- Manage coach placement pipeline
- Create marketing campaigns and coupons
- View revenue and performance reports
- Message coaches and parents

### Future AI features
- Optimal batch scheduling based on coach/venue availability
- Coach-student matching algorithm for batch assignment
- Enrolment forecasting and demand prediction
- Automated parent progress report generation
- Competitive analysis with peer academies

## Parent

### Profile
A parent or guardian of a young athlete (typically under 18). Wants visibility into their child's sports development.

### Goals
- Monitor child's training progress and performance
- Ensure child is attending scheduled sessions
- Communicate with coaches about development
- Discover quality coaching and academies for their child
- Manage payments and contracts for their child

### Pain points
- Limited visibility into what happens during training
- No objective measure of child's improvement
- Hard to coordinate with coaches and academies
- Concerns about safety and supervision
- Managing multiple children's schedules

### Daily workflow
1. Open app → view child's dashboard and upcoming sessions
2. Check attendance and coach feedback from recent sessions
3. Review performance metrics and progress reports
4. Respond to messages from coach or academy
5. Manage payments and view contracts

### KPIs
- Child's session attendance rate
- Performance metric trends
- Coach ratings and feedback
- Achievement milestones
- Communication responsiveness

### Success metrics
- Child's improvement trajectory
- Session attendance consistency
- Coach-parent communication quality
- Platform engagement (check-ins per week)

### Main screens
- **Dashboard**: Child's KPIs, upcoming sessions, recent activity, coach messages
- **Progress**: Performance charts, attendance history, achievement timeline
- **Coach**: Coach profile, communication, feedback history
- **Schedule**: Child's session calendar, booking management
- **Payments**: Fee history, contracts, payment methods
- **Settings**: Manage linked children, notification preferences

### Permissions
- View linked children's profiles and data
- Schedule and cancel sessions for their child
- Communicate with coaches
- View and approve contracts
- Make payments
- View performance metrics and attendance

### Future AI features
- Personalized progress report generation
- College/sports scholarship recommendations
- Optimal training load monitoring for injury prevention
- Coach communication sentiment analysis
- Comparative performance benchmarking (anonymous)

## Venue Owner

### Profile
Owner or manager of a sports facility (ground, court, pool, gym) who lists their venue for booking.

### Goals
- Maximize venue utilization and revenue
- Manage bookings and availability
- Build venue reputation through ratings
- Attract more customers (players, coaches, academies)

### Pain points
- Manual booking management and conflicts
- No-show customers without payment
- Difficulty marketing venue availability
- Limited visibility into utilization trends

### Daily workflow
1. Open app → view today's bookings
2. Confirm or manage booking requests
3. Update venue availability and pricing
4. Review venue ratings and feedback

### KPIs
- Booking fill rate (utilization %)
- Revenue per venue per month
- Customer rating
- Repeat booking rate

### Success metrics
- Utilization rate target (70%+)
- Customer satisfaction (rating 4.5+)
- Revenue growth
- No-show reduction

### Main screens
- **Dashboard**: Utilization KPIs, upcoming bookings, revenue chart
- **Bookings**: Booking calendar, manage requests, check-in/check-out
- **Venue**: Venue profile, photos, amenities, pricing, availability
- **Reviews**: Customer ratings and feedback
- **Earnings**: Revenue dashboard, payout history

### Permissions
- Create and manage venue profiles
- Set pricing and availability
- Accept/reject bookings
- Check in customers
- View earnings and reports
- Respond to reviews

### Future AI features
- Dynamic pricing based on demand and seasonality
- Optimal scheduling to maximize utilization
- Predictive maintenance alerts
- Customer segment analysis for targeted marketing

## Admin

### Profile
Platform operator who manages users, content, and system health.

### Goals
- Ensure platform safety and compliance
- Manage user disputes and reports
- Oversee platform growth and health
- Generate business intelligence reports

### KPIs
- Active users (DAU/MAU)
- Transaction volume and value
- User satisfaction (ratings, reviews)
- Platform stability (uptime, error rates)
- Support ticket resolution time

### Main screens
- **Dashboard**: System KPIs, active users, revenue, recent issues
- **Users**: User management, role management, ban/suspend
- **Reports**: Business intelligence, usage analytics, financial reports
- **Content**: Community moderation, reported content
- **Support**: Support tickets, dispute resolution
- **System**: Feature flags, maintenance mode, configuration

### Permissions
- View all users and their data
- Moderate all content
- Manage user roles and permissions
- Access all reports and analytics
- Manage system configuration
- Resolve disputes and support tickets
- Manage coupons and promotions globally
- Override any platform restriction

### Future AI features
- Automated content moderation
- Fraud detection in bookings/payments
- User churn prediction and intervention
- Intelligent support ticket routing and auto-resolution
- Growth opportunity identification from usage patterns
