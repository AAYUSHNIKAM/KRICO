# First: Is the idea good?

The strongest part of your idea is not session booking.

The strongest part is:

**Player ↔ Coach ↔ Parent ↔ Academy ecosystem.**

Most sports apps focus only on:

* booking
* attendance
* payments

Very few focus on creating a sports network where:

* players showcase progress
* coaches build reputation
* academies recruit coaches
* parents track development
* players find practice partners

That is where the long-term value is.

    final now = DateTime.now();
